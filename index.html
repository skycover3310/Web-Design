Biography

Chen-Yao Yu (artist name: SkyCover) is a composer and pianist working across film, television, animation, game audio, and new media. He graduated from the Music and Piano Performance program at TNUA(Taipei National University of the Arts), developing a strong foundation in classical music and an international perspective. He has participated in exchange performances at the Shanghai Conservatory of Music and Musashino Academia Musicae in Japan, and received recognition from the WOCMAT International Young Computer Music Composer Award.

His creative work spans commercial television, independent games, animation, film, and interdisciplinary interactive art. In music production, he combines orchestral writing with contemporary electronic sound design to shape the emotional and dramatic language of visual media. With a strong background in piano performance, he also works in piano arranging and recording. His approach continues to explore diverse sonic languages and provide music solutions for a wide range of visual and multimedia projects.
