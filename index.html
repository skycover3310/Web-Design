余辰曜

作曲家｜鋼琴家｜聲音設計

個人簡介

余辰曜（藝名為 SkyCover）是一位活躍於影視、動畫、遊戲與新媒體展演的跨界配樂家與鋼琴家，畢業於國立臺北藝術大學音樂系鋼琴演奏組，具備深厚的古典音樂底蘊與國際視野，曾赴上海音樂學院與日本武藏野音樂大學交流演出，並獲 WOCMAT 國際青年電子音樂創作獎肯定。其作品涵蓋商業電視、獨立遊戲、動畫與電影，其中《Sky Maker》入選 SPARK ANIMATION 2026，作品亦曾入圍金穗獎、布宜諾斯艾利斯國際影展（BUEIFF）等國際影展。

他的創作版圖涵蓋商業電視連續劇、獨立遊戲與跨域互動藝術；在幕後配樂製作上，他擅長將管弦樂編制與現代電子合成聲響融合並刻畫影像張力；同時，他也憑藉扎實的主修底子，提供高品質的鋼琴編曲與錄音實戰能力。他持續以多元的聲響語彙，為各類視覺載體提供專業且高執行力的音樂解決方案。

Yu Chen Yao

Composer | Pianist | Sound Designer

Biography

Chen-Yao Yu (artist name: SkyCover) is a composer and pianist active across film, television, animation, game audio, and new media performance. He graduated from the Piano Performance program at Taipei National University of the Arts, where he developed a strong foundation in classical music alongside an international perspective. He has participated in exchange performances at the Shanghai Conservatory of Music and Musashino Academia Musicae in Japan, and received recognition from the WOCMAT International Young Computer Music Composer Award.

His creative work spans commercial television, independent games, animation, film, and interdisciplinary interactive art. In music production, he combines orchestral writing with contemporary electronic sound design to shape the emotional and dramatic language of visual media. With a strong background in piano performance, he also works in piano arranging and recording. His approach continues to explore diverse sonic languages and provide music solutions for a wide range of visual and multimedia projects.
